Fitness app · Subscription model

Onboarding to paid, a concept flow

This prototypes the MVP-1 features from my PRD for a fitness-app subscription model. The PRD has no wireframes, so the screens are my interpretation: a 2-minute onboarding quiz, a sample personalised plan, a 14-day limited trial, Basic / Premium / Super tiers, and a plan that adjusts from feedback.

Concept prototype, not a real app, no payment is taken

Flow

    "Simulate day 14" is enabled once the trial starts. Every plan, workout and meal is a sample built from simple rules on your quiz answers. The PRD's success metrics (quiz completion, trial-to-paid conversion, retention after the first month, adoption of AI personalisation) are not measured here, since nothing is tracked.
