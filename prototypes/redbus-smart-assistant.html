Smart Travel Assistant

When a destination is not directly served by buses, a traveller can ask in plain language and the assistant suggests the nearest reachable stop and bus-plus-local-ride combinations, which can be sorted by cheapest, fastest or most comfortable. This prototype walks one example trip, Bangalore to Kudremukh, from the case study.

Concept prototype, not the real app, nothing is booked

Flow

    Try the quick options or type something like "I want to go to Kudremukh". The two routes are the illustrative example from the case study. Bus details, amenity icons and the map are sample placeholders, not real timetables, operators, ratings or fares.
