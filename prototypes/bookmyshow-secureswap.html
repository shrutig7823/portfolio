Ticketing case study · Live events

SecureSwap

Before booking opens for a high-demand live event, a user verifies identity by linking a government-issued ID. Verified fans can book up to 4 tickets and share them with friends who are also verified, so bots and scalpers are filtered out. If plans change, tickets can be returned, pooled and passed to other verified users. Tap through both flows on the right.

Concept prototype, not the real app, no ID or payment is collected or sent

Flow

    Screens follow the wireframes in the case study. Event, venue, dates, seats and prices are sample values. The document buttons only mark a document as added: no file is opened, read or stored. Verification is described in the case study as taking around 24 hours; here it finishes after a few seconds or when you press Finish verification.
