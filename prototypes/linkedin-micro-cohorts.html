Case study concept · Student-first network

Micro-cohorts prototype

My case study deck has no wireframes, so this prototypes the features I proposed in it: Goal-Based Micro-Cohorts, the Cohort Wall, and the Progress Tracker with Check-ins (all Must Have), plus a simple version of Smart Peer Matchmaking (Should Have). The idea: a clutter-free place to learn and build in small cohorts, with no noise, no algorithm, just real progress with real peers.

Concept prototype, not the real app, no data is stored or sent

Flow

    All people, posts, goals, dates and numbers are sample content made up for this demo. Members appear as initials only. Posts you write stay in this page's memory and disappear on reload. Public profile pages and recruiter access are deliberately left out, as in my Won't Have list.
