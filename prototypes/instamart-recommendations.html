Smart recommendations at the cart

Concept from my case study on raising order value per store. When an item is out of stock, an AI suggests a similar one. As you build a cart it also suggests add-ons, and a rewards screen shows what adding recommended items could unlock. This follows my wireframes; the items, prices and reward rules are sample values.

Concept prototype, not the real app, no order is placed

Flow

    Try it: add items, then open the trophy to see the reward tiers from the wireframe. Items, prices, the delivery and handling lines, and how each reward applies are illustrative. The wireframe does not say what the 10% discount applies to; this prototype applies it to the recommended items only. Cashback is shown as paid after the order, not taken off the bill. The "9 orders" prize needs order history, so it is not simulated.
