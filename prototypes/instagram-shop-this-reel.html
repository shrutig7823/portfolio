Shop This Reel

On a creator's Reel, a Shop This Reel button opens a panel of the products in that Reel. The shopper picks a product, checks the details and size, then taps through to buy from the external retailer, with no public comment and no waiting for a DM. The purchase itself is completed on the vendor's site.

Concept prototype, not the real app, nothing is sent or bought

Flow

    Tap inside the phone to move through the flow. Creator, products, prices and sizes are sample content for illustration only.
